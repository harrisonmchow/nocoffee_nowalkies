# Surface brief: Home (with Photos and Work pages)

Scope: Home `/`, Photos `/photos`, Work (UGC portfolio) `/work`. The mode for Home and Photos is Experience, with the places leading. Work is Persuade, aimed at brands.
Audience: followers of @nocoffee_nowalkies first, and brands evaluating UGC work second.
Action: followers browse places and photos. Brands email nocoffeenowalkies@gmail.com.
Content: stand-in photos and drafted copy, labelled as such, until the group supplies real material. Do not invent brand names, metrics or testimonials.
Constraint: SKILL.md (minimalist-ui) is binding for palette, type and materials.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the trackhead sign at the start of every walk. Five fingerposts, one per region, are the navigation and the identity. This replaces the default arrangement of a hero, a tagline and a photo grid.

OWN-WORLD: Warm bone ground (#F7F6F3), charcoal ink (#111111) and muted grey (#787774), with 1px #EAEAEA sign borders. The fingerpost is a crisp rectangle (radius 4px or less) with a pointed end drawn as an authored SVG, an editorial serif name, mono coordinates and a bold arrow. Pastel grade-style tags are pale green, yellow, blue and red. Photography is desaturated and warm. One continuous hairline trail line threads every page (raise from the neon challenger).

STORY: The visitor sees where these four go, picks a place, sees it through the group's photos, meets Harry, Kevin, Caleb and Mikey, and (if they're a brand) understands the UGC offer and emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed photo of the active region. Bottom left, the region name is set as one enormous serif word that never shrinks (raise from the TDR challenger). Right column, a stack of five fingerpost signs with name, state or country, coordinates and an arrow. Hovering, focusing or tapping a sign crossfades the photo and swaps the big word; this is the signature interaction. Slim top nav: wordmark, Places, Photos, Work, and an Instagram link.

FORM: Australian national park trackhead signage, candidate 3 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 7c83c0ac. Raise from the Du Bois challenger: a to-scale latitude figure plotting all five regions (31°S to 42°N).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
